About

Academic and entrepreneurial experience

Chaos Engineering was founded in 2023 by Justin Dirrenberger, a researcher and lecturer specialising in architected materials.

Associate Professor (HDR) at the Conservatoire national des arts et métiers (Cnam) in Paris and junior member of the Institut universitaire de France, Justin Dirrenberger has more than 15 years of experience in research and innovation, from the design of architected materials to their additive manufacturing and the modelling of their mechanical behaviour.

He has co-founded several technology companies, including XtreeE, which specialises in additive manufacturing for construction, and TETMET, which develops lattice structure manufacturing. He has led many research projects funded by national and European agencies.

Chaos Engineering brings this experience to start-ups and innovative companies, to inform their technical choices and to structure their research and development projects.
